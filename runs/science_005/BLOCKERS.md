# Blockers

- No sources were unreachable. All three target families were cached with source URLs and access dates.
- Target B exact reproduction was limited to n <= 8 by exhaustive local verification cost. For n=9..13, the n=8 interval was reused as a valid lower bound but not a reproduction of the Hagedorn exact values.
- Target C was limited to small prime gaps below 20000 and produced no record-table-relevant gap. The result is a small science curve, not a competitive prime-gap search.
